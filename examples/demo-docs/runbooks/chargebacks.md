# Runbook: chargebacks

- A chargeback must be answered within 7 days of the notice, or it is lost by default.
- Disputes under $25 are accepted automatically; do not contest them.
- For disputes of $25 or more, attach the order record, delivery proof and customer emails, then submit through the disputes portal.
- More than 1% of monthly orders charged back puts the account in the card network's monitoring program. Tell the risk team (risk@) as soon as the rate passes 0.8%.
