# Incident severity

| Level | Meaning                              | First response                         |
| ----- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| SEV1  | Customers cannot pay at all          | 5 minutes, page the incident commander |
| SEV2  | Payments degraded or one region down | 15 minutes                             |
| SEV3  | Internal tools or reports broken     | Next business day                      |

A SEV1 needs a written postmortem within 5 business days. SEV2 needs one only if it lasted more than 2 hours.
